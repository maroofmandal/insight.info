---

Insight.info starts with a simple belief: analytics is most valuable when it shortens the distance between a question and a product decision.

Most growing teams need two kinds of context. Web analytics explains where attention comes from. Product analytics explains what people accomplish after they arrive. Keeping those views separate creates duplicated setup and incomplete stories, so Insight.info puts them in one workspace.

## One focused analytics workspace

Insight.info combines web traffic, product behavior, users and funnels in a single focused experience with readable defaults and shareable dashboards.

The initial release introduces a single-origin experience at `insight.info`, including readable public dashboard links such as `insight.info/example.com?t=24hrs`.

## A deliberately smaller footprint

Our product direction emphasizes purposeful event collection, legible controls and dashboards that answer common questions without excessive configuration. Privacy is not a slogan or an automatic legal conclusion; it is a set of engineering and product choices that should be visible to the people making them.

## What comes next

The first release focuses on installation, traffic, events, users, journeys and funnels. We will document changes as they ship and keep improving the path from activity to useful answers.
