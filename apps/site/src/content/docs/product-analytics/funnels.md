---
title: Funnels
description: Measure completion and drop-off across an ordered sequence of events.
category: Product analytics
order: 14
---

Create a funnel from two or more events, such as `pricing_viewed`, `checkout_started` and `subscription_created`. Choose a time range that matches the natural decision cycle.

Treat a drop-off as a prompt to investigate. It does not prove why people stopped, so pair the funnel with journeys, support feedback and product research.
